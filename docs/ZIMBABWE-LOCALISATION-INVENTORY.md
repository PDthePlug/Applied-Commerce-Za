### What the counts mean

- **Rand / R examples** are the largest mechanical workload, but they are not suitable for a blind currency conversion. Values must be rebuilt around credible Zimbabwean household, school, work and business situations.
- **Stokvel** is concentrated in the early financial-behaviour material. Some contexts can become *mukando* / savings-club examples, but the surrounding social logic must still be checked.
- **Spaza** and **taxi rank** occur across the enterprise pathway. These scenes need full Zimbabwe setting passes rather than isolated noun replacement.
- **Matric, NSFAS and SARS** become important in the later transition material. These require a Zimbabwe-specific post-school, funding and tax rewrite.
- **South African locations** should only be moved when the whole scene remains coherent after localisation.

## Editorial priority

### P0 — Structural
- Re-sequence four source terms into Zimbabwe's three-term school rhythm.
- Complete the Form 4 / Form 5 split instead of leaving the temporary bridge.
- Map each unit to its intended HBC competency / project outcome.

### P1 — Learner-critical factual context
- Currency and pricing examples.
- Tax, employment and formal-economy references.
- Tertiary funding and post-school pathways.
- School-stage language and examination transitions.
- Any legal or public-institution claim.

### P2 — Story and economic environment
- Locations.
- Informal trade and transport settings.
- Stokvel / mukando contexts.
- Spaza / neighbourhood trade contexts.
- Household and community examples.

### P3 — Language polish
- Zimbabwean vocabulary where it improves authenticity.
- Teacher-facing instructions and school terminology.
- Repetition and any South African assumptions that remain after factual localisation.

## Completion rule

A source book is **not** considered adapted merely because these counts reach zero. It is complete only after:

1. three-term placement is assigned;
2. Zimbabwe economic examples read naturally;
3. factual financial/tax/funding claims are verified;
4. recurring story continuity is intact;
5. HBC capability mapping exists;
6. learner tasks still produce meaningful evidence;
7. curriculum, language, UI, typecheck, lint and build checks pass.

## Recommended conversion order

1. **Form 1 / source Grade 8** — establish the Zimbabwe voice, money model and mukando/community-economy conventions.
2. **Form 2 / source Grade 9** — enterprise and work contexts.
3. **Form 3 / source Grade 10** — value creation and systems.
4. **Form 3 / source Grade 11 Terms 1–2** — localise leverage and leadership as the advanced bridge into the final year.
5. **Form 4 / source Grade 11 Terms 3–4 + Grade 12** — rebuild wealth, legacy, matric/NSFAS/SARS-era transitions around Zimbabwean O-Level school-leaving, tertiary, work and enterprise realities.
